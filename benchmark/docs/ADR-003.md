# ADR 003: Progressive disclosure for the APx explainer

Status: accepted for the APx interactive Site.

## Context

The first APx experience opened with a strong visual metaphor and exposed the formula early. It still required visitors to infer the core meaning of the unit. APx introduces two related values, AP and APx, plus evidence, quality, cost, safety and human supervision. Presenting all of them at once raises comprehension cost.

The primary audience includes founders, operators, researchers and policy leaders who need to understand the business meaning before reading the measurement protocol.

## Decision

Teach APx in four layers.

1. State the question: how much finished, approved work did the agents produce?
2. Show one concrete comparison: eight human hours versus one complete agent workflow hour equals 8 AP and APx 3.
3. State three invariants: same job, accepted output only and full workflow time.
4. Reveal the formula, interactive calculator, evidence model and benchmark harness as the visitor continues.

Keep the Three.js instrument as the memorable visual anchor. Place the plain language example directly after the hero. Preserve explicit labels on illustrative values so the teaching example cannot be mistaken for measured telemetry.

## Alternatives considered

| Choice | Comprehension speed | Precision | Main risk |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Formula first | Low | High | Excludes nontechnical readers |
| Metaphor only | Medium | Low | Visitors infer different meanings |
| Calculator first | Medium | Medium | Inputs appear before the unit is understood |
| Example then formula | High | High | Adds one section to the narrative |

## Consequences

The opening becomes longer, but visitors can understand the unit before encountering logarithms or evaluation protocol details. The example describes throughput for one matched task. It does not imply universal worker replacement.

Mobile layout stacks the human, agent and score panels vertically. The arrows rotate to preserve the reading sequence. Reduced motion behavior and the existing 3D fallback remain unchanged.

## Rollback

The introduction is isolated as one semantic section and its styles use dedicated class names. It can be removed without changing the calculator, scoring kernel or benchmark contract.

## Acceptance tests

A first time visitor should be able to answer these questions after the new introduction: what APx measures, what 8 AP means, why APx 3 represents 8 AP, and why runtime alone is insufficient.
