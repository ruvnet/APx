# ADR 002: Evidence bound acceptance and fail closed scoring

Status: accepted for the APx reference implementation.

## Context

APx converts accepted outputs into human reference hours. A weak acceptance path can therefore inflate every downstream metric. Runtime logs, artifact hashes, automated tests and reviewer statements each prove different things. None alone proves that a deliverable satisfied the declared task.

The scorer must also remain useful when evidence is incomplete or when a known safety failure exists. Returning a numeric score in those cases creates false precision and makes unsafe comparisons appear valid.

## Decision

Count an output only when its evidence binds the task slot, artifact identity, evaluator identity, run identity and acceptance decision. Measured evidence requires an independent reviewer declaration. Reject conflicting artifact aliases and repeated deliverables so one result cannot earn credit more than once.

Any known safety failure makes the aggregate result ineligible. AP and APx become null while diagnostic counts, elapsed time, cost and human labor remain visible. Unknown safety does not earn output credit.

Promotion requires the candidate to beat the frozen parent without safety, task quality or human labor regression. Human review person hours cannot be lower than the review wall time component. Direct inputs reject reserved identifiers, cyclic values and excessive nesting before canonical hashing.

## Alternatives considered

| Choice | Cost | Benefit | Failure mode |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Trust declared acceptance | Low | Simple ingestion | Self reported success inflates AP |
| Trust artifact hashes | Low | Strong identity | Integrity does not prove correctness |
| Trust automated tests only | Moderate | Repeatable checks | Incomplete tests miss task intent and safety |
| Bind evidence and require independent adjudication | Higher | Replayable accepted output | Reviewer quality remains an external dependency |

## Consequences

Measured pilots require more operational work than activity metrics. The result is easier to audit because every credited hour traces to an exact accepted artifact. Incomplete evidence produces an ineligible or uncertified result instead of a misleading number.

The largest remaining risk is evaluator capture or correlated error. Production certification therefore needs reviewer qualification, conflict disclosure, sampled second review and periodic blind adjudication audits outside this package.

## Rollback

Keep immutable manifests, receipts and scorer versions. A disputed result can be replayed against the prior scorer. Changes to evidence fields or acceptance policy require a new schema version and cannot silently reinterpret old results.

## Acceptance tests

The implementation must reject duplicate aliases, unrelated adjudication receipts, missing reviewer independence, known safety failures and promotion candidates that increase total or campaign human labor.
